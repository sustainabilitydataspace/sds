# Target Onboarding & Access Flow (SSI/VC)

**Version:** 2026-04-13
**Status:** Active

This target flow describes a future SSI/VC onboarding option. It is not a
currently enabled SDS access path.

> Runtime enforcement hold: this is a target flow, not a currently enabled VC
> access-control path. No issuer is activated for automated enforcement until
> verified custody/provenance evidence and a fail-closed runtime verifier are
> approved. Current connector policy enforcement remains limited to the runtime
> controls actually implemented and validated by SDS.

## Actors
- Organization (legal entity)
- Prospective issuer after activation
- Consumer or provider connector
- Provider EDC (policy enforcement point)

## Steps
1. Registration & KYC/KYB
   - Organization submits identity evidence to a trusted issuer.
   - Issuer verifies the organization and prepares an `OrgIdentityCredential`.
2. Issuance (OIDC4VCI)
   - Organization requests issuance; issuer signs and returns the `OrgIdentityCredential`.
   - If needed, issuer also issues an `SDSRoleCredential` bound to the connector DID.
3. Connector Registration
   - Organization deploys an EDC-compatible connector and registers its DID.
   - Organization associates the connector DID with its organizational credential.
4. Trust Anchors (future activation)
   - Provider may populate `docs/governance/trust_issuers.md` only after the
     runtime verifier and required custody decisions are activated. The current
     registry is intentionally empty and cannot authorize access.
5. Contract Negotiation & Presentation (OIDC4VP; future activation)
   - Connector requests a data product; Provider EDC challenges for VPs.
   - Connector presents VPs (`OrgIdentityCredential` + `SDSRoleCredential`).
   - Provider EDC will validate signatures, temporal validity, status credentials,
     issuer trust, and claim constraints through the approved runtime verifier.
6. Policy Evaluation & Access
   - Provider EDC evaluates policies (purpose, region, retention) per product terms and policy registry.
   - If permitted, contract is concluded and access is granted; obligations (LOG and retention) are enforced.
7. Audit & Rotation
   - Access and policy decisions are logged; revocations and rotations are handled via Bitstring Status List 2025 and trust-list updates.

## Notes
- Maintain minimal claims; avoid PII in credentials unless strictly necessary.
- Keep any future issuer registry under version control and review quarterly
  after activation.
- Evidence for onboarding decisions is captured in the E6 evidence package generated by `scripts/e6_generate_evidence.py`.
