# SDS public synthetic demo

Everything in this directory is deterministic, invented public demonstration
data. It is not derived from Nordhaven records, exports, identifiers, logs, or
dumps. UC-01 through UC-03 demonstrate persisted energy, emissions and water
observations plus compatible-unit conversion. UC-04 through UC-06 are bounded
storage/metadata data-contract demonstrations only.

It does not demonstrate emissions-factor or inventory calculations, water-risk
assessment, policy enforcement, connector control, circularity optimisation,
ESG scoring, lending or credit decisions, territorial or legal admissibility,
certification, or compliance.
